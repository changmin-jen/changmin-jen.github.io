About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Electronics & Computer Engineering at Seokyeong University. My research focuses on reliable post-training and inference-time methods for large language models, with particular interest in reinforcement learning, reward overoptimization, and test-time alignment.

I am interested in understanding how language models can make better decisions under imperfect reward signals and limited inference budgets.
